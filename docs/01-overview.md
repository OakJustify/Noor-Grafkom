## 1. General Description

Noor is a Python-based game development project that focuses on education about Alzheimer's disease. The player plays as a character who has Alzheimer's, but the player is not told directly that the character has Alzheimer's. Through the gameplay experience, players are expected to understand the symptoms, difficulties, and needs of people with Alzheimer's, so that empathy grows and negative stigma decreases.

Noor is an educational medium, not a diagnostic tool, therapy, or substitute for professional medical consultation.

## 4. B. Background & Context

**OVR-007 — What is the background of the project idea?**
Answer: Many people in society do not yet understand Alzheimer's correctly. The symptoms that appear are often misinterpreted as crazy or insane, so people with the disease and their families experience social stigma.

**OVR-008 — What condition or situation encouraged the creation of this project?**
Answer: People with Alzheimer's are often underestimated, ostracized, and not treated seriously. More engaging and interactive educational media are needed, especially for children and young adults.

**OVR-009 — Are there previous projects, products, studies, or systems that serve as references?**
Answer: No formal references have been recorded yet. References that can be used are Alzheimer's educational materials, Alzheimer's awareness campaigns, and educational narrative games. This section still needs to be completed by the team.

**OVR-010 — Why is this project relevant to develop now?**
Answer: Stigma against people with Alzheimer's still often occurs. Interactive game media can be a more engaging way to build empathy. This project is also relevant as the final assignment for the Computer Graphics course with the application of pycairo and pygame.

## 5. C. Problem Statement

**OVR-011 — What is the main problem to be solved?**
Answer: Negative views and stigma against people with Alzheimer's who are considered crazy or insane, so they are underestimated and ostracized.

**OVR-012 — Who experiences this problem?**
Answer: People with Alzheimer's, their families and caregivers, and the general public who do not yet understand this disease.

**OVR-013 — How does this problem occur in the current condition?**
Answer: The lack of education about Alzheimer's makes symptoms such as forgetting, confusion, or behavioral changes misunderstood as mental disorders or strange behavior.

**OVR-014 — What is the impact of this problem?**
Answer: People with the disease and their families experience stigma, social isolation, shame, lack of support, and treatment that is not serious.

**OVR-015 — Why are currently available solutions not sufficient?**
Answer: Existing educational media tend to be passive, such as articles or seminars. Games can provide an interactive experience and empathy through the player's role as a character with Alzheimer's.

## 9. G. Project Scope

**OVR-029 — What is included in the project scope (in-scope)?**
Answer: A Python-based desktop game, use of pygame for the game loop, input, window, and audio, use of pycairo for drawing game assets, educational narrative gameplay about Alzheimer's, assets, source code, documentation, a demo, and a build that can be run locally.

**OVR-030 — What is not included in the project scope (out-of-scope)?**
Answer: Medical diagnosis or treatment, clinical claims, multiplayer, online backend, cloud save, user analytics, native mobile applications, multi-language localization, and professional medical validation.

**OVR-031 — What are the main limitations of the project?**
Answer: The project is an academic assignment, runs as an offline desktop game, uses Python with pygame and pycairo, and is developed by a small team.

**OVR-032 — Are there limitations in time, cost, resources, or technology?**
Answer: Time is limited to the course deadline. The budget is minimal or without a special budget. Resources consist of a small team and limited development devices. Technology is limited to Python, pygame, pycairo, and desktop.

**OVR-033 — Are there features or capabilities planned for the future but not included in the current version?**
Answer: Mobile version, multi-language, advanced accessibility modes, additional levels, cloud save, player analytics, and validation with medical experts. These features are not included in the current version.
