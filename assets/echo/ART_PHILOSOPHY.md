# Silt Resonance

ECHO's original artwork treats abandoned infrastructure as the remains of a
signal. The visual algorithm contrasts organic recursion with measured,
engineered repetition: trees lose thickness at each fork while the floodworks
repeat rivets, drainage bars and tower braces. Meticulously crafted constraints
keep this opposition readable rather than merely decorative.

Seeded variation bends branches within narrow angular limits and distributes
reeds around an undulating bank. The same seed always reconstructs the same
place. Deliberate gaps are as important as occupied space; distant silhouettes
lead toward the hollow signal tower without competing with the courier.

Depth emerges from independent parallax velocities, cool grayscale luminance
steps and overlapping translucent fog bands. These layers are generated once
through Cairo and reused as textures. The painstaking optimization is part of
the aesthetic: atmospheric motion remains calm and continuous, never burdened
by full-frame vector reconstruction.

The courier's light is the smallest, clearest shape in the composition. A round
chest lantern and forked scarf belong to an original asymmetric rain-hood
silhouette. Carefully posed walking and airborne frames turn the body's movement
into a readable rhythm, while the pale cyan glow persists in darkness.

Music shares the landscape's mathematics: periodic low-frequency modes hold a
space open, filtered stochastic wind erodes its edges, and a sparse original
bell phrase occasionally resolves into silence. Visual grain and acoustic
texture are subordinate to that stillness. This master-level implementation
aspiration is a design discipline, not a claim of borrowed authorship: every
shape, texture, waveform and motif is synthesized locally for this project.
